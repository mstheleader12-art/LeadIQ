How LeadIQ 2.0 works

A simple explainable lead-prioritisation workflow.

1. Data input
Sales team enters prospect profile, intent, engagement, source and recency.

2. Scoring engine
A transparent weighted formula calculates a score from 0 to 100.

3. Classification
HOT (75–100), WARM (50–74), or COLD (0–49).

4. AI explanation and chatbot
When configured, Gemini generates natural-language explanations and answers questions through the backend. The key stays on the server.

5. Recommended action
LeadIQ suggests a next step based on priority.

Scoring weights
Profile fit: 20 · Buying intent: 35 · Engagement: 25 · Source: 10 · Recency: 10.

Important: This prototype uses a rule-based score, not a trained predictive model. It does not represent a verified probability of conversion.
